Layer 1: 16 feature maps (28×28)

Each 5×5 filter responds to simple patterns: edges at different angles, stroke ends, thick or thin lines.

Layer 2: 32 feature maps (14×14)

Combinations of layer-1 patterns: curves, corners and loops, at half the resolution.
